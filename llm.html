Architectural Overview

The Transformer Block Pipeline

Modern large language models process input text by passing dense, high-dimensional vectors through a series of identical transformer decoder blocks. Within each block, information flows through self-attention computation and non-linear mapping steps:

1. Self-Attention (Information Aggregation): Multi-Head Attention projects incoming representations into query, key, and value vectors. It computes relative correlation weights stochastically, allowing token states to aggregate context from surrounding context.
2. Feedforward & MoE (Mapping & Routing): The aggregated states pass through a multi-layer feedforward neural network (FFN) to perform non-linear mapping. In Mixture of Experts (MoE) topologies, a gating router dynamically shifts tokens to specialized FFN sub-experts.
