Architectural Overview

Verticalization: Specialized Domains

General-purpose language models struggle in high-stakes domains due to a lack of deep, proprietary vertical data and an inability to deal with complex domain-specific tasks safely. Verticalization solves this by combining specialized models with rigid validation and operational workflows:

1. Core Model Fine-Tuning: Take a highly capable general model, apply continued pre-training on vertical publications, and execute supervised fine-tuning (SFT) using curated task pairs designed by industry domain experts (clinicians and security analysts).
2. Grounding & Planning Frameworks: Protect vertical applications from logical drift by sandwiching the domain model between authoritative intelligence datastores (RAG grounding) and API orchestration tools.
